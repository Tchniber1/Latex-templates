AMATH301 Exam Revision Guide - build instructions

Requirements: TeX Live 2023 or newer with XeLaTeX (tcolorbox, pgfplots, tikz, scrlayer-scrpage,
fontspec, mathpazo, TeX Gyre Pagella). Montserrat is bundled in fonts/, no install needed.

Build (run from this folder, three passes for the table of contents and references):

    xelatex main.tex
    xelatex main.tex
    xelatex main.tex

Files
  main.tex        master file (cover, structure, order of chapters)
  style.tex       palette, fonts, boxes, thumb tabs, figure styles, macros
  front.tex       How to use this guide, course map, answer templates
  ch1.tex..ch5.tex  Lectures I to V: theory, TD problems solved, exam-style problems, quick checks
  toolkit.tex     theorems table, proofs, strategy, traps, MCQ drill
  mock.tex        Mock Exam A and Mock Exam B with marked solutions
  annexe.tex      Lectures VI and VII
  formulas.tex    formula sheet
  beautybook.cls, stys/, cncolours.sty   template (Overleaf beautybook) and stubs
  inner_pics/, figs/   chapter mark and logo (PDF) with their TikZ sources

Palette: #64CADE #6DD2E7 #95E1F1 #F19381 #FFB1A1, text shades #1F7F92 #145F6E #C95A46.

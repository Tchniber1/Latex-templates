AMATH301 Mock Exam, Explained - build instructions

Requirements: TeX Live 2023 or newer with XeLaTeX (tcolorbox, pgfplots, tikz, scrlayer-scrpage,
fontspec, mathpazo, TeX Gyre Pagella). Montserrat is bundled in fonts/.

Build from this folder, three passes:
    xelatex main.tex
    xelatex main.tex
    xelatex main.tex

Files: main.tex (structure), style.tex (palette, boxes, tabs, plots), front.tex (Before You Start),
p1.tex, p2.tex, p3.tex (one chapter per problem), wrap.tex (summary and tick sheet),
beautybook.cls + stys/ + cncolours.sty (template), fonts/, inner_pics/, figs/.
